Approach

Language-first systems thinking

My linguistics background helps me define what an AI system should understand before deciding how it should be implemented.

I work from ambiguous business questions toward explicit definitions, labels, rules, metrics, formulas, prompts, and workflows. That includes deciding what counts as an issue, how sentiment should be interpreted by aspect, when a signal becomes a crisis, and what evidence a generated insight must use.

I then translate those definitions into working AI-assisted systems using structured prompts, database-connected tools, reusable analytical skills, and validation logic.

DefineTurn vague concepts into operational labels, metrics, and decision rules.
DesignBuild prompts, workflows, taxonomies, state machines, and analytical components.
ValidateUse baselines, false-alarm controls, evaluation datasets, and traceable evidence.
